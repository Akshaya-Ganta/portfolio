A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Code meets
creative worlds.

I like working at the intersection of programming, visual design and interactive systems.

My approach is hands-on and prototype-driven. I work mostly independently, write the majority of the gameplay scripts in my projects, and learn by building, testing and iterating.

I've explored more than one game engine and enjoy both the logic behind an interaction and the atmosphere of the world around it. Beyond games, I build XR, 3D web and hardware-connected experiences.
